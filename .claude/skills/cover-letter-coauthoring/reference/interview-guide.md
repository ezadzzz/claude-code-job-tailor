# Context Interview Guide

Conduct conversational interviews to gather authentic stories. Ask follow-up questions and follow interesting threads. The goal is authentic conversation, not a rigid checklist.

## Interview Categories

### 1. Company Connection & Motivation

**Opening:** "Why this company? What drew you to this role specifically?"

**Follow-ups:**
- Used their products/services?
- Specific project or initiative that excites you?
- How did you first hear about this opportunity?
- Something about their mission/values that resonates?
- Personal connection to the industry/problem?

**Look for:** Authentic stories demonstrating genuine interest, not generic enthusiasm.

### 2. Achievement Stories (Most Critical)

**Goal:** Identify 3-5 achievements relevant to the role with quantifiable metrics.

**Questions:**
- Which past achievements best demonstrate the top job requirements?
- Tell me the story behind [achievement from resume]
- What's your proudest accomplishment related to this role?
- Have you solved problems similar to what they're facing?

**For each story, dig deeper:**
- Specific metrics/results? (MUST be quantifiable)
- Timeline?
- Your specific role vs. team contribution?
- Obstacles overcome?
- Skills used that apply to this role?

**Every story must include:**
- Context (situation)
- Challenge (what needed solving)
- Action (what YOU did)
- Result (quantifiable: numbers, percentages, revenue, time saved)

### 3. Non-Traditional Background (if applicable)

If career transition, gaps, or unconventional path:

**Questions:**
- What motivated the transition?
- How have you been building skills in this area?
- Independent projects or learning?
- How does previous experience strengthen your candidacy?

**Goal:** Turn potential weakness into differentiation story.

### 4. Personal References & Networks

**Question:** "Do you have any connections at [Company]?"

**If yes:** Get name, title, relationship, permission to mention
**If no:** Note this - don't fabricate

### 5. Company-Specific Fit

**Question:** "Why are you uniquely suited for THIS role at THIS company?"

**Probes:**
- What specific problem they're solving matches your skills?
- How does your experience align with their challenges?
- What unique perspective do you bring?
- How do your values align with their culture?

### 6. Tone Preferences

**Questions:**
- How formal vs. conversational?
- Any phrases or words to avoid?
- Address any concerns proactively? (gap, location, etc.)
- How much personality should come through?

### 7. Open-Ended Context

**Question:** "Anything else that would help me write a compelling letter?"

**Examples:**
- Side projects, volunteer work
- Unique skills, certifications
- Awards, recognition
- Published work, speaking
- Hobbies demonstrating relevant skills

## Synthesis Before Drafting

After gathering context, summarize and confirm:

```
Here's what I've learned:

**Company Connection:** [Their authentic story]

**Top Achievement Stories:**
1. [Story + quantifiable result]
2. [Story + quantifiable result]
3. [Story + quantifiable result]

**Unique Fit:** [Why specifically suited]

**Tone:** [Preference]

Does this capture everything? Anything to add or change?
```

**Only proceed to drafting when user confirms context is complete.**
